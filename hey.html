A private communiqué

Mr. Sagar.

There exists a matter upon which I have been unnecessarily contemplative.

01 / An acknowledgement

I owe you an apology, albeit one considerably overdue.

I shall refrain from manufacturing an elaborate justification for circumstances in which my own conduct may have contributed to unnecessary turbulence.

Whatever our present circumstances may signify, I retain an unfeigned regard for the person you are and for the history we happen to share.

02 / A proposition

Were you afforded precisely one benevolent concession toward me, what would you choose?

03 / Concluding remarks

No expectations. No hidden clauses.

I merely wished to place an overdue apology somewhere beyond the limitations of an ordinary conversation.

Whatever designation our relationship presently possesses, I sincerely hope life treats you with considerably more kindness than my occasionally questionable judgement has managed to.

🐶

After all that unnecessarily sophisticated vocabulary...

I simply wanted to say: take care, Sagar. — Kajal
